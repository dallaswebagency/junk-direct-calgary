Volume pricing

Pay only for the space you use

Pricing is based on how much of our 16-foot dump trailer your load fills. If your items take up a quarter of the trailer, you pay for a quarter load. When a load falls between tiers, we always round in your favour.

Minimum

A few boxes or a single piece of furniture.

Quarter

A closet clear-out, a few appliances or a small room.

Half

Garage cleanouts, estate items or a moderate pile of household junk.

Three-quarter

Basement or attic cleanouts and larger projects.

Full

Complete home cleanouts, renovation debris or large commercial jobs. Our best value.

FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Do you offer same-day service?

Yes, we offer same-day service for many of our customers. Contact us early in the day to book.

Do I need to lift anything or prepare?

No. Our team handles all the sorting, loading and hauling. No preparation is needed.

Do you recycle or donate items?

Whenever possible, yes. Donation drop-offs and recycling are prioritized to reduce landfill waste and benefit local charities.

Can you help with estate cleanouts?

Yes. We handle partial or full estate cleanouts and work respectfully with families and executors, including out-of-town executors managing Calgary properties remotely.

What is the minimum price for junk removal?

There is no fixed minimum. You pay only for the space your items take up on the truck. Free estimates are offered, and curbside pickup starts from $89.

What do you not take?

Like most Calgary providers, we do not accept hazardous materials such as asbestos, oils, chemicals or solvents. Propane tanks, car batteries and some lithium-battery electronics are also typically excluded. Call us if you are unsure about an item.
